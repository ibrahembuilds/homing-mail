Homing

Send outreach. Get replies back.

The idea

Homing pigeons were the first reliable messengers: let one go and it finds its way home. Homing is built around the same promise. Your emails go out, and the replies come back to one inbox. The mark is a paper plane leaving home, and the dots are the route it remembers. The tile shades from iris to green, the sheen on a pigeon's neck.

Logo

Keep clear space of half the tile's width on every side. At favicon sizes (under 48 px) the dotted trail is dropped, leaving the plane and the home dot. Files are in branding/static/; regenerate them with make_icons.py.

Colour

Iris is the action colour: buttons, links, the active tab. Neck green appears only in the logo sheen and in success states. Night is the app's sidebar and sign-in page.
