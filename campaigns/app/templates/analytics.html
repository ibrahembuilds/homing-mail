How to read these numbers

  • Open rate is an upper estimate. Apple Mail and some security tools load images automatically, which counts as an open nobody made. "Confirmed human" excludes those and is a lower estimate. Use clicks to compare campaigns.
  • Bounce rate should stay under 2%. A higher rate means the list has old or mistyped addresses; campaigns pause themselves above 8%.
  • Spam complaints counts people who pressed "report spam" at providers that tell senders (Yahoo, Outlook and others once you enrol in their feedback loops). Gmail never reports individual complaints: watch your Gmail rate in Google Postmaster Tools and keep it under 0.1%.
  • No sender can see whether a message landed in the inbox or the junk folder. A falling open rate at one provider in the table above is the usual sign of junk-folder placement there.
